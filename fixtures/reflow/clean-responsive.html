Welcome to the harbour town

The town grew around its harbour, which still lands fish every morning. The old quarter, the market and the beaches are all within a walk of the station, and buses go up the hill to the castle every twenty minutes.

Markets

Fish market every morning, food market on Saturdays in the square.

Beaches

Three beaches, two of them watched by lifeguards in summer.

Walks

The coast path and the river walk start from the harbour wall. Details: https://example.org/walks/coast-path-and-river-walk
