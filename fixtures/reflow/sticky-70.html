Waste collection

Household waste is collected once a week, on the day shown for your street. Put the bin out the evening before, with the lid closed, and bring it in once it has been emptied.

Recycling is collected every other week. Paper, cardboard, cans and plastic bottles go in the blue bin; glass goes to the bottle banks found in every car park of the town.

Garden waste is collected from March to November for those who have subscribed to the service. Branches longer than a metre and soil are not taken.

Bulky items such as furniture and appliances can be collected on request, for a fee that depends on the number of items. Some of them can be given to the reuse centre instead.

Collections on public holidays move to the next working day. The calendar for the year is sent to every household in December and can be downloaded from this page.

Missed collections can be reported up to two days after the collection day. A crew comes back within three working days if the bin was out in time.

Bins that are damaged or stolen are replaced free of charge once a year. Larger bins are available for households of five people or more.

The recycling centre on the ring road is open every day but Christmas. Bring proof of address: it is for residents only, and vans need a permit.
