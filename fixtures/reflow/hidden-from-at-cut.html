Offer

Resident permits are half price until the end of the month for every household in the controlled zones.

Visitor permits are sold in books of ten at the town hall and at the library desk.

Paragraph 1. Residents of the controlled zones can apply for a permit for each car registered at their address, up to two per household.

Paragraph 2. Residents of the controlled zones can apply for a permit for each car registered at their address, up to two per household.
