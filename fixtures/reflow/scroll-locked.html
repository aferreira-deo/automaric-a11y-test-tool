Sign up for the newsletter

Once a month we send the programme of the theatre, the concerts in the park and the exhibitions of the museum, with the dates on which tickets go on sale and the offers for subscribers.

Your address is used for the newsletter and for nothing else. It is never given to anyone, and every message has a link that takes you off the list at once, without asking why.

The newsletter is written by the culture service of the town and sent from its own servers. Past issues can be read on the site before you sign up.

Associations of the town can send their own events to the culture service before the fifteenth of each month; they are listed in the next issue, free of charge, with a link to their own pages.

Subscribers who prefer paper can collect a printed copy of the programme at the library, the tourist office and the town hall from the first day of each month.
