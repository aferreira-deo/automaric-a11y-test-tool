Parking permits

Residents of the controlled zones can apply for a permit for each car registered at their address, up to two per household. The permit is valid for a year from the date it is issued.

Visitors can be given day vouchers, sold in books of ten at the town hall or online. A voucher is valid on the day written on it, in the zone of the resident who gave it.

Businesses in the zones can apply for a permit for vehicles needed for their work. Proof of the activity and of the registration of the vehicle is asked for.

Blue badge holders park free of charge in every zone and in the car parks of the council, for as long as they need.

Permits are renewed online a month before they end. A reminder is sent by email to those who gave an address when they applied.

A lost or stolen permit can be replaced once a year for a small fee. Report it first, so that the old permit cannot be used.

Changes of vehicle are free: give the registration of the new one and the permit is updated the same day.
