This guide explains how to deploy the service to a cluster and keep it running across upgrades without downtime for its users. Its sections are numbered, and none of the numbers is a figure.

1 Introduction

The service is shipped as a single container image that reads its settings from the environment when it starts.

1.2 Requirements

You need a cluster with at least three nodes and a recent version of the command line client on your machine.

2. Installation

The installer downloads the image layers on its first run, so it needs access to the network at that point.

3D rendering options

The renderer can be switched between the software path and the hardware path with one flag when it starts.

5G coverage map

The map shows where the service can be reached from a phone, region by region, and how fast it answers there.

2026 roadmap

The roadmap lists the features planned for the year and the order in which they are expected to reach users.
