A router that looks at every click

What listens to every click on this page sets the same 3 s timer whatever is clicked; when it runs, it goes to the address of the link clicked if that link is one of the app's (a.later), and does nothing otherwise. The links of the app show a spinner and then go: they are links. The link that only shows a panel acts as a button.
